That error is straightforward:

Microsoft.Cdn is not registered for the subscription
means your Azure subscription hasn’t enabled the CDN resource provider yet.

✅ Fix (Portal – easiest for demo)
Go to Azure Portal
Search → Subscriptions
Select your subscription
Go to Resource providers
Search: Microsoft.Cdn
Click Register
